## Scientific sources

- Vavilov, N. I., expeditions across Central Asia, 1920s–30s — the hypothesis that the Tian Shan and Trans-Ili Alatau region is the centre of origin of the cultivated apple.
- Velasco, R. et al., "The genome of the domesticated apple (*Malus × domestica* Borkh.)", *Nature Genetics*, 2010 — genetic confirmation of Sievers' apple as the main wild ancestor of cultivated varieties.
- Dzhangaliev, A. D. — research on studying and conserving Kazakhstan's wild apple; see the [Dzhangaliev](../dzhangaliev/) page.
- Ainabekov, M. S., Turekhanova, R. M., Ivashchenko, A. A., "Kuznetsov gorge" genetic reserve — [articlekz.com](https://articlekz.com/article/8869) (in Russian).
