## Flowering season

The trail is especially picturesque in spring, during the flowering of Sievers' apple. In April–May, the mountain slopes are covered in delicate white-pink blossoms, creating a unique atmosphere for eco-tourism, photography, and educational excursions.

The spring flowering of Sievers' apple is one of the region's major natural events, and could become a striking eco-tourism symbol for Kazakhstan.

The bloom itself is brief — usually just one to two weeks, from late April to early May — so it's worth checking the exact dates before planning a visit.
