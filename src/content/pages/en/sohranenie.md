## Conserving Sievers' apple

To conserve this rare species, a 31.9-hectare forest genetic reserve was established in 2010 in the Kuznetsov gorge of the Malovodnoye forestry, Turgen branch.

Since 2019, a 0.7-hectare clone archive has operated within the park, preserving valuable forms of Sievers' apple.

Each year, around 28,000 seedlings are planted across 15 hectares. Up to 120 kg of seeds are also collected annually and sown in nurseries to grow further planting stock.
