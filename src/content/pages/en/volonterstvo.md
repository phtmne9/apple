## Partner support

On June 19, the Kuznetsov gorge saw the opening of the Sievers' apple educational nature trail and the Aimak Dzhangaliev museum. The event marked the 30th anniversary of Ile-Alatau National Park.

The project was carried out in partnership with the Helios company, and is aimed at developing environmental education, promoting Sievers' apple, and preserving Kazakhstan's natural heritage.

As early as October 2024, a specialised Centre for the Study and Conservation of Sievers' Apple was established under the Institute of Botany and Phytointroduction, within the Ministry of Ecology and Natural Resources of the Republic of Kazakhstan — giving further momentum to infrastructure development on the park's protected territory.
