## Volunteer programmes

Ile-Alatau National Park runs conservation activities aimed at restoring and preserving Sievers' apple.

Each year, around 28,000 Sievers' apple seedlings are planted across 15 hectares of the park. Up to 120 kg of seeds are also collected annually and sown in nurseries to grow further planting stock.

Where possible, visitors, volunteers, and eco-tourists can take part in planting seedlings, caring for young trees, and environmental campaigns. Information about such events is published in advance on the national park's official pages.
